At Thanh's: it's all in the family. Vietnamese cuisine is in our heart and our soul since 3 generations.

The foundation starts with Mrs Thanh's father and mother who opened "Thanh Thế" restaurants in the 1st district of Saigon in Vietnam. The story goes on when the Thanh's family seeks refuge in France in 1975. They opened then "Le Vieux Saïgon" in Bastille area in Paris.

Today the 3rd generation carry on the tradition in Luxembourg with Thanh's Boulevard Royal and Thanh's Infinity. Opened in 2009, Thanh's Boulevard Royal was the first authentic vietnamese place in Luxembourg.
