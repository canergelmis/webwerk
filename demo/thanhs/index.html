  1. Saigon · 1st district

    Mrs Thanh's father and mother open the “Thanh Thế” restaurants.

  2. France · 1975

    The family seeks refuge in France. Later, near Bastille in Paris, they open “Le Vieux Saïgon”.

  3. Luxembourg · 2009

    Thanh's Boulevard Royal opens: “the first authentic vietnamese place in Luxembourg”.

  4. Kirchberg · 2023

    A second room opens: Thanh's Infinity. Today the 3rd generation carries on the tradition.
