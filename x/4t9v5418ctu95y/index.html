How the plays and the "Top 5 in" column work
  • Localise first when English proficiency is below EF's "High" band (EF EPI 2025 score under 550). Lead lever: relevance.
  • Monetise when today's use is at or above the median of the 20 markets and the full-price tier applies. Lead lever: payments and price.
  • Price for reach when use is at or above the median but a discount or entry tier applies. Lead lever: payments and price.
  • Activate when use is below the median. Lead lever: the market's biggest fixable barrier.
  • The rules run in that order. Tier settings in the market brief can change a play.
  • Business plays follow the barrier that weighs most in a country's mix compared with the EU-27 mix (Eurostat 2025), because lack of expertise is the most cited barrier almost everywhere: unclear legal consequences or data protection gives Trust-led; lack of expertise gives Enablement-led; incompatible systems or data problems give Integration-led; cost gives Self-serve. Where barriers aren't published the motion is open. The target segment is the firm size with the most staff not yet using generative AI.
  • Focus markets are the five largest by population.
  • Top 5 in: the share of 5,000 random weightings, spread evenly over every way to split 100% across the five factors, under which the market ranks in the top 5. It does not depend on your weights. Hover a cell for the median rank and range.

    Consumer adoption today

    Who isn't using it yet

    Generative-AI use by age, work, education, place and sex, and how many people in each age band have not used it. This is where awareness, skills and relevance campaigns aim.

    How it's used

    Which sectors' firms use AI, and what people ask an AI assistant for in this market.

    Price

    The global list price, adjusted by the market's price level (PPP conversion factor over the exchange rate), then banded into tiers. Tier settings apply to every market.

    Tier cut-offs and shares are assumptions. Change them.

    Funnel

    Start from the people already online and today's generative-AI use, then apply your own rates to reach paying subscribers and revenue at the tier price above. The last three rates apply to every market and feed the Europe roll-up.

    Every rate here is an assumption, not data. The defaults are round placeholders.

    Business funnel

    From the seat pool (staff in firms not yet using generative AI) to paid seats. Every rate and the seat price are assumptions; the seat price is not localised.

    Growth levers

    Score each lever 0 (not started), 1 (in progress) or 2 (done). The first four groups answer the reasons people give for not using generative AI; the group matching this market's largest fixable reason is marked. Scores are kept per market in this browser only.
